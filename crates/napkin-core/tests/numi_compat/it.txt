# Phrases of Numi's Italian vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
conversion.variants	per	`per` multiplies between two numbers (`3 per 4`) and divides before a unit (`60 km per ora`); conversions use `in`, `a`, `come`
hour.variants	o	`o` is the conjunction 'or' (`3 o 4 persone`); hours are `ora`/`ore`/`h`
nautical_mile.variants	nm	`nm` stays the nanometre; nautical miles are `mn`/`miglia nautiche`
cbrt.variants	radice quadrata	`radice quadrata` is the square root; the cube root is `radice cubica`
tablespoon.variants	cucchiaini	`cucchiaini` are teaspoons; tablespoons are `cucchiai`
divide.variants	gl'	a fragment of `diviso gl'` split by a stray comma; a bare article cannot divide
as_a_percent_on.variants	come un percentuale di aumento dello come un percentuale di aumento dell'	two phrases run together in Numi's list; each works on its own
SCR.variants	rupie	a fragment of `rupie delle Seychelles` split by a stray comma; a bare `rupie` is the Indian rupee
