# Phrases of Numi's English vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
are.variants	a	`a` is the article (`5 a day`, `as a % of`); ares are `are`/`ares`
thousand.variants	th	would turn ordinals into thousands (`5th` = 5000)
thousand.variants	th.	would turn ordinals into thousands (`5th.` = 5000)
teaspoon.variants	t.	`t` is the tonne; `5 t.` at the end of a sentence must stay 5 tonnes
