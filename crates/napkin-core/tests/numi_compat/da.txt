# Phrases of Numi's Danish vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
acre.variants	hektar	`hektar` is the hectare (10,000 m²), not the acre (4,047 m²); acres are `acre`
hour.variants	t	`t` stays the tonne; hours are `time`/`timer`/`h`
teaspoon.variants	t.	`t` stays the tonne; teaspoons are `teske`/`tsk`
tablespoon.variants	teskeer	`teske` is the teaspoon; tablespoons are `spiseske`/`spsk`
tablespoon.variants	tsk	`tsk` is the teaspoon; tablespoons are `spiseske`/`spsk`
tablespoon.variants	T.teske	two phrases run together in Numi's list; `T.` and `teske` each work
cbinch.variants	c.i.CI	two phrases run together in Numi's list; `c.i.` and `CI` each work
AMD.variants	dram	a dram is a shot of spirits in Danish; `armensk dram` works
AMD.variants	Dram	a dram is a shot of spirits in Danish; `armensk dram` works
