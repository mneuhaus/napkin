# Phrases of Numi's Dutch vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
conversion.variants	met	Numi lists `met` as plus and as conversion; Napkin reads it as plus (`5 met 3`); convert with `naar`, `in`, `als`
multiply.variants	ver	`ver` is Dutch for far (`5 km ver`); `vermenigvuldigd met`, `maal`, `keer` work
are.variants	a	`a` is the article in English and `à` (to) in Dutch ranges (`2 a 3 dagen`); ares are `are`/`aren`
nautical_mile.variants	nm	`nm` stays the nanometre; nautical miles are `NM`/`zeemijl`
carat.variants	K	`K` stays the kelvin; carats are `karaat`/`ct`
carat.variants	kt	`kt` stays the knot; carats are `karaat`/`krt`
tonne.variants	TNT	a ton of TNT is energy (4.184 GJ), not a mass; tonnes are `ton`/`t`
centner.variants	centenaar	a centenaar is 100 pond (50 kg), Napkin's centner is 100 kg (`kwintaal`, `qtl`)
centner.variants	cn	abbreviation of `centenaar` (50 kg), Napkin's centner is 100 kg (`kwintaal`, `qtl`)
ounce.variants	ons	a Dutch `ons` is 100 g; Napkin reads it as a hectogram; ounces are `ounce`/`oz`
ounce.variants	os	abbreviation of the Dutch `ons` (100 g); ounces are `ounce`/`oz`
pint.variants	pintje	a pintje is a 25 cl beer glass, not a pint (473 ml); pints are `pint`
pint.variants	ptje	abbreviation of `pintje` (a 25 cl beer glass), not a pint; pints are `pint`
pint.variants	imperial pint	the imperial pint is 568 ml, Napkin's pint is the US pint (473 ml)
pint.variants	imperial pintje	the imperial pint is 568 ml, Napkin's pint is the US pint (473 ml)
cbinch.variants	KD	`KD` is the Kuwaiti dinar (KWD); cubic inches are `kd`/`kubieke duim`
AZN.variants	AM	`3 AM` is three in the morning; `manat` works
AZN.variants	A.M.	`3 A.M.` is three in the morning; `manat` works
BIF.variants	BR$	Numi lists `BR$` under BND too; Napkin reads it as BND; `Burundese frank` works
BSD.variants	B$	Numi lists `B$` under BND too; Napkin reads it as BND; `Bahamaanse dollar` works
NIO.variants	C$	`C$` is the usual sign of the Canadian dollar; Napkin reads it as CAD
EGP.variants	L£	Numi lists `L£` under LBP too; Napkin reads it as LBP; `LE`, `Egyptisch pond` work
SCR.variants	SR	Numi lists `SR` under SAR too; Napkin reads it as SAR; `SRe` works
TMT.variants	manat	Numi lists `manat` under AZN too; Napkin reads it as AZN; `Turkmeense manat` works
KMF.variants	FC	`FC` is the Congolese franc (CDF) in every language; `Comorese frank` works
MWK.variants	MK	`MK` stays the megakelvin; `Malawi-kwacha` works
BTN.variants	Nu.	`nu` (now) ends many Dutch sentences; `ngultrum` works
LAK.variants	kip	Dutch for chicken (`500 g kip`); `Laotiaanse kip` works
UZS.variants	sum	`sum` is the sum of the block above; `Oezbeekse sum` works
CUP.variants	CUC	CUC is the convertible peso, its own code
CUP.variants	convertibele peso	the convertible peso is CUC, not the Cuban peso (CUP)
CUP.variants	Cubaanse convertibele peso	the convertible peso is CUC, not the Cuban peso (CUP)
GBP.variants	pond	`pond` is the weight on lines without money, like `pound`; `pond sterling`, `Britse pond`, `£` work
BTC.variants	B||	`||` is an operator; bitcoins are `bitcoin`, `BTC`, `₿`
FKP.variants	£%@FKP	a format template, not a phrase
FKP.variants	£%@ FKP	a format template, not a phrase
GIP.variants	£%@GIP	a format template, not a phrase
GIP.variants	£%@ GIP	a format template, not a phrase
MGA.variants	FMG	the Malagasy franc (FMG) gave way to the ariary in 2005 (5 FMG = 1 MGA), a code Napkin does not know
STD.variants	dobra	STD is the dobra before 2018 (1000 STD = 1 STN), a code Napkin does not know; the name reads as today's STN
VEF.variants	Bs.F	VEF, the bolívar fuerte, gave way to VES in 2018 and is a code Napkin does not know (`Bs` alone is the boliviano)
SVC.variants	El Salvador colon	the Salvadoran colón (SVC) gave way to the US dollar in 2001 and is a code Napkin does not know
SVC.variants	El Salvador cólon	the Salvadoran colón (SVC) gave way to the US dollar in 2001 and is a code Napkin does not know
