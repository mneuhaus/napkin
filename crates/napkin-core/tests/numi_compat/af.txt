# Phrases of Numi's Afrikaans vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
conversion.variants	met	Numi lists `met` as plus and as conversion; Napkin reads it as plus (`5 met 3`); convert with `na`, `in`, `as`
are.variants	a	`a` is the English article (`5 a day`, `as a % of`); ares are `aar`/`are`
byte.variants	g	`g` stays the gram; bytes are `B`/`greep`
mile.variants	m.	`m` stays the metre; miles are `myl`
minute.variants	m.	`m` stays the metre; minutes are `min`/`minuut`
million.variants	m.	`m` stays the metre (`2 m` is two metres); millions are `miljoen`/`mil.`
teaspoon.variants	t.	`t` stays the tonne; teaspoons are `teelepel`
pint.variants	pt.	`pt` stays the typographic point; pints are `pint`/`pinte`
gallon.variants	g.	`g` stays the gram; gallons are `gelling`/`gell.`
nautical_mile.variants	sm.seemyl	two phrases run together in Numi's list; `sm.` and `seemyl` each work
cbinch.variants	kub. dm.	`dm` is the decimetre, so this reads as cubic decimetres; cubic inches are `kubieke duim`/`kd`
