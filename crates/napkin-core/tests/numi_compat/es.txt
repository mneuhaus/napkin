are.variants	a	`a` is the preposition and converts (`5 km a millas`); the are is `área`
inch.variants	"	`"` opens a quoted comment in Napkin; inches are `pulgadas`, `in` or `″`
thousand.variants	miles	`miles` after a number stays the English mile (`5 miles`); Spanish counts thousands with `mil`
thousand.variants	Miles	`miles` after a number stays the English mile (`5 miles`); Spanish counts thousands with `mil`

percent_off.variants	de	`de` is listed for both of and off; `20 % de 50` is a share of 50 (10), a discount is `de descuento sobre`
percent_on.variants	en	`en` is the conversion word (`5 km en millas`); a share on top of a base is `sobre`
