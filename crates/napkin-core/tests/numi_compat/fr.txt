# Phrases of Numi's French vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
thousand.variants	m	`m` stays the metre (`2 m` is two metres); thousands are `mille`/`milliers`
thousand.variants	m.	`m` stays the metre (`2 m` is two metres); thousands are `mille`/`milliers`
thousand.variants	milles	`milles` is the plural of the mile (`3 milles marins`); the numeral `mille` never takes an s
nautical_mile.variants	mn	`mn` is the everyday French abbreviation of the minute (`10 mn`); nautical miles are `milles marins`/`m.n.`
GBP.variants	pound	`pound` follows English: the weight, and the pound sterling on lines with money (like `livre`)
