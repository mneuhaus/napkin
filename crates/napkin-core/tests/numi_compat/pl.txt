# Phrases of Numi's Polish vocabulary that Napkin reads differently on purpose.
# key<TAB>phrase<TAB>reason
are.variants	a	`a` is the Polish conjunction ("and", "but") and the atto prefix; ares are `ar`/`ary`/`arów`
inch.variants	'	`'` is the foot mark and the Swiss thousands separator, not inches
foot.variants	st.	`st` is the stone symbol; feet are `stopa`/`stopy`/`stóp`
week.variants	t	`t` is the tonne symbol; weeks are `tydz.`/`tyg.`/`tydzień`
year.variants	r	`r.` follows every year in Polish prose (`12 marca 2026 r.`, `w 2026 r.`), so it is not a unit; years are `rok`/`lata`/`lat`
cup.variants	łyżka	mistranslation: `łyżka` is the tablespoon (and reads so); a cup is `szklanka`
cup.variants	łyżki	mistranslation: `łyżka` is the tablespoon (and reads so); a cup is `szklanka`
cup.variants	łyżek	mistranslation: `łyżka` is the tablespoon (and reads so); a cup is `szklanka`
percent_of.variants	procent	Numi lists `procent` as the word for `%` and as "of"; it stays the percent sign (`20 procent z 50`)
percent_of.variants	procenty	Numi lists `procenty` as the word for `%` and as "of"; it stays the percent sign (`20 procent z 50`)
percent_off.variants	od	mistranslation: `20% od 50` is "20% of 50" in Polish (`podatek 19% od dochodu`), so `od` reads as `of`; "off" is `odjęte od`/`rabatu od`/`mniej niż`
BYR.variants	rubel białoruski	BYR is an obsolete code; Napkin reads the Belarusian rouble as BYN
BYR.variants	ruble białoruskie	BYR is an obsolete code; Napkin reads the Belarusian rouble as BYN
BYR.variants	biał. rubel	BYR is an obsolete code; Napkin reads the Belarusian rouble as BYN
