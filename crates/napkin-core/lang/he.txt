# Hebrew (עברית). Format: see crates/napkin-core/src/vocab.rs.
# Word units are shown in the CLDR plural categories one | two | other. With a numeral Hebrew
# uses the plural for two as well (`2 שעות`; the dual `שעתיים` already means "two hours"), so
# the two and other forms are the same. Abbreviations are written with gershayim (״) or a plain
# double quote (ק״מ, ק"מ) and geresh (׳) or an apostrophe (אינץ׳, אינץ'); both are listed.
# One-letter prefixes joined to the next word (ב, ל) are listed where they matter: `בלונדון`,
# `במרץ`, `לשעה`.
