# Chinese in traditional characters (繁體中文), as written in Taiwan and Hong Kong. Format: see
# crates/napkin-core/src/vocab.rs.
#
# Chinese is written without spaces: every character is a token of its own and the longest
# known phrase wins (`5公里`, `5千克`, `5千` is 5000). Characters this file does not know stay
# prose. Numbers are written in digits; Chinese numerals (`二十`) are prose. A bare `元` or `塊`
# is the New Taiwan dollar, as in Taiwan (Hong Kong writes 港元/港幣, Macau 澳門元).
