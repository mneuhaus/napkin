# Turkish (Türkçe). Format: see crates/napkin-core/src/vocab.rs.
# Turkish uses the singular after numbers, so every word unit has one form (`3 saat`).
# Phrases of three or more characters match any case through Rust's lowercasing, which maps
# `I` to `i` and `İ` to `i̇`: words with ı, İ, ş, ğ, ç, ö, ü also come without the marks
# (`yil`, `dolari`), which covers text typed on keyboards without Turkish letters and in capitals.
