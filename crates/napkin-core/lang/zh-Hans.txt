# Chinese is written without spaces: every character is a token of its own and the longest
# known phrase wins (`5千米` is 5 km, `5千` is 5000, `5千克` is 5 kg). Characters this file does
# not know stay prose. Numbers are written in digits or Chinese numerals (`二十`, `三百五十`).
