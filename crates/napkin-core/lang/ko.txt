# Korean (한국어). Format: see crates/napkin-core/src/vocab.rs.
#
# Korean words are separated by spaces and break at digits (`5센티미터`, `3억 원`). Particles
# written onto a word are part of it, so the topic forms of common units and currencies are
# listed (`5인치는 몇 센티미터`, `9달러는 몇 유로`), and the possessive forms of money
# (`30000원의 15%`). Object and direction particles (`달러를`, `유로로`) are left out: without
# a conversion word between them, two units would multiply.
#
# Units come before `now`: `시간` is hours after a number and the current time alone.

# Arithmetic. `의` puts the base first (`50의 20%` is 20% of 50, so it multiplies). There is no
# `per` word: `당` and `마다` follow the unit they divide by (`km당 100원`).
plus = 더하기, 플러스, 더한
minus = 빼기, 마이너스, 뺀
times = 곱하기, 곱, 곱한, 의
divide = 나누기, 나눈
convert = 몇, 을, 를
convert-after = 으로, 로
assign = 은, 는
